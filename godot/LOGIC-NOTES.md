## Drone in wind (wind lane, 2026-10-23)
Owner's aim: in strong, gusty wind the drone visibly and audibly works harder, wobbles and drifts, and stays flyable. Calm by default.
- **Inputs:** `Wind.sample` (unchanged: log profile, slow gusts, fast flutter) gives `quad.wind`. Then `Wind.drive(quad, terrain, pos, agl, dt)` runs once per physics tick (main.gd `_physics_process`, after the sample) for the drone only.
- **Turbulence at the drone:** `sigma = intensity(agl) x profile(agl) x (1 + shelter)`. `shelter` (0..1) = woodland cover (`Terrain.cover_at`) 8, 20 and 40 m upwind, faded out above 8 to 40 m AGL; in the lee the mean wind drops 35% and the turbulence doubles. `burst` (0..1) is a slow noise for gust spells; `spell = 0.55 + 0.9 burst`.
- **Gust puffs (drift):** a 2.5 s Ornstein-Uhlenbeck vector added to `quad.wind`, size `GUST_H x sigma/(1+sigma/6) x spell x susc`. Position hold brings the drone back.
- **Eddy torque (wobble):** body torque `quad.gust_tau` = smooth 0.15 s two-stage noise x `TORQUE_K x rho x Cd x A x arm x soft(|v_rel| + sigma) x sigma x spell x susc`, soft limit `x/(1+x/10)` so a storm stays flyable. Physics divides by inertia, so light drones are thrown more. `susc = (0.6/mass)^0.35` (Mini 1.36, Cinema 0.74) is the owner's "light drones much more than heavy" direction.
- **Rotor flapping:** a gust change (wind high-passed at 0.5 s, in the body frame) tips the drone downwind for a moment: `FLAP_K x m g R` per m/s.
- **Weathervane (crab):** side airspeed gives a small yaw torque (centre of side pressure `VANE_L x arm` behind the centre); the yaw loop's I term stops it after a few degrees, so the nose sits a little into the wind.
- **Position hold** (`flight_controller.gd`): Normal and Sport, sticks centred, stopped (under 0.6 m/s): holds the spot, flies back at up to 2.5 m/s (gain 0.9 N, 0.6 S). Released by a stick, landing, or a jump over 2 m (reset/teleport). Blown over 20 m away: the spot is dragged along. `saturated`: the velocity loop asked for more than the bank limit. `losing_hold()`: saturated and over 3 m off the spot. `tilt_error()`: uncommanded tilt (test metric).
- **Off switch:** Weather tab "Wind effects on the drone: Off / Natural" (`hud.wind_fx`, signal `wind_fx_changed`), sets `wind.struggle`; Off zeroes `gust_tau` and skips puffs and shelter (old model exactly). `quad.gust_tau` defaults to zero, so the physics parity test is unchanged.
- **Motor strain:** comes from the physics (uneven RPM while the controller fights the torque; more thrust and power in strong wind). Sound (`drone_audio.gd`, `strain_in`): each motor's loudness follows its own speed, a soft triangle whine at the electrical frequency (7 pole pairs) when a motor runs over 1.06 to 1.4 x its hover speed, plus rough-air rumble on the onboard microphone (Buffet bus) from `turb x burst`.
- **Camera shake** (`game/shake.gd`, `CamShake`, applied in main.gd after the first-person camera is placed, Full camera only, not in Simple): smooth noise in time (9/s buzz + 2.5/s knock) scaled by `turb x (0.45 + 0.55 burst)`, eased over 0.3 s. Gimbal: 0.6 deg x level plus a 4 ms gimbal lag on body rates, capped 1.5 deg. FPV: 1.6 deg x level, capped 4 deg (the frame wobble itself is already in the picture). Also adds to the sensor `vibration` (jello). Setting "Camera shake: Off / Natural".
- **Warnings** (`Wind.feedback`, called each frame from main.gd): wind at the drone smoothed over 1.5 s. Over the airframe's comfortable wind (`COMFORT`: Mini 8, Freestyle/Race 12, Cinewhoop 9, Cinema 14 m/s): "Strong wind". Losing the hold for 1.5 s: "Wind too strong to hold position". Shown as a quiet amber line on the display and beside the WIND readout; a notice (`hud.toast`) at most once per level per windy spell; a spell ends after about 20 s well under the limit.
- **Test:** `test/wind_test.gd` (run in two parts, it is slow: `-- ids=mini,cinema` and `-- ids=freestyle,race,cinewhoop`): 60 s hover at 10 m in five winds per airframe; asserts calm wobble under 0.3 deg, storm wobble RMS under 6 deg, no crash, Mini more disturbed than Cinema, jolt recovery under 0.7 s, take off, hover and land with the sticks in strong, storm and strong storm, warnings. Dev tokens in a `shot=` run: `windms<m/s>` (menu wind, gusty), `hover` (after `sky<n>`, stops the auto climb), `nowindfx`; and `windlog` prints the struggle and shake once a second.
## Trees (realism 3.1, lane/trees, 2026-10-23)
- **Models** (`tools/tree_gen.ts`, run through `tools/export_assets.ts --trees-only`; it writes `assets/trees/*.glb`, the leaf atlases `assets/trees/leaves_*.webp`, `assets/textures/bark_birch_diff.jpg` and the tree entries of `assets/manifest.json`, leaving `env/all.glb` alone). Nine species, own procedural work: `spruce_a`, `spruce_b` (Norway spruce: whorls of sprig strips plus hanging curtains), `maple_red`, `maple_gold`, `oak`, and new `beech` (wide dome), `birch` (narrow, white bark, drooping sprays), `poplar` (tall column), `pine` (Scots pine: bare stem, orange upper bark, flat crown of needle tufts). Broadleaf crowns are an ellipsoid of 10 to 15 clumps; each clump gets a main limb from the trunk, sub-branches, and clusters of three folded (V-shaped) leaf cards at random turns. Baked into COLOR: occlusion from depth in the crown and in each clump (lit tops, dark cores) and, in alpha, how near the outside a card is. UV2 keeps (phase, flex) for the wind. Normals on leaves are crown-shaped, so a crown lights as one volume.
- **Detail levels**: LOD0 4,400 to 5,950 triangles (budget 6,000), LOD1 450 to 720 (same clumps, fewer and bigger cards from the atlas's dense cell, so volume and colour hold), far silhouettes 42 (conifer) and 96 (broadleaf: three lumps at different heights). - **Materials** (`game/assets.gd`, search `## ---- trees`): models carry no textures; materials are named `bark:<set>` and `leaf:<atlas>` and `Assets._tree_mat` builds one shared ShaderMaterial per name. Tables `BARK` (file, metres per repeat, tint, desaturate, moss) and `LEAF` (translucency, roughness, tint, snow hold) hold the look. Older models without named materials still fall back to `foliage.gdshader`.
- **Shaders**: `tree_common.gdshaderinc` (the same hierarchical sway and rotor wash as `foliage.gdshader`, plus the per-tree variety), `tree_leaf.gdshaderinc` body with `tree_leaf.gdshader` (desktop: alpha to coverage, needs the scene's MSAA) and `tree_leaf_mobile.gdshader` (touch devices and non-Forward+ renderers: alpha scissor; chosen in `_tree_mat`). Leaves: alpha scaled up with the mip level so distant crowns do not thin out, back faces use the crown normal (`if (!FRONT_FACING) NORMAL = -NORMAL`, no black leaves), BACKLIGHT deeper in colour than the albedo and stronger at the crown rim, snow on upper outer leaves, darker and glossier when wet. `tree_bark.gdshader`: photographed bark at real size with its normal map, moss low on the north side, wet furrows, snow on limb tops. `tree_far.gdshader` (far silhouettes): per-copy lumps, height and width, colour lean, specular off; its tint is sRGB (converted in the shader, as the old material colour was).

## Fireworks (new experiences E1, lane/fireworks, session B, 2026-10-23)
Owner's aim: a real firework show over the lake that can be started from the pause menu and flown through, with light, smoke and sound that arrive in the right order.
- **Where:** `game/fireworks.gd` (class `Fireworks`, one node built in `main.gd` after the sound), shaders `game/shaders/firework_spark.gdshader`, `firework_spark_draw.gdshader`, `firework_smoke.gdshader`, `firework_smoke_draw.gdshader`, `firework_flash_draw.gdshader`. The barge and shell models come from `NewModels.barge()`, `NewModels.barge_tubes()` and `NewModels.firework_shell_proxy()`; nothing is modelled in this lane.
- **Barges and shells:** three barges on the lake at about (-812, 68), (-792, 100), (-812, 132), origin at the waterline, water level `Lake.LEVEL` 76 m. Each has the 20 mortar tubes from `NewModels.barge_tubes()`. A show event is one burst (time, type, position, colour set, size); the shell is launched `rise` seconds earlier (4.2 to 5.1 s) with a ballistic velocity that reaches the burst point exactly at its time. A gold spark trail follows the shell (particle mode 7) and the shell proxy is drawn while it rises.
- **Bursts:** eight types, two pooled `GPUParticles3D` emitters each (so a burst is one draw call): peony, chrysanthemum (long streaks), willow (droop), palm (9 arms), ring, heart, crackle (tiny blinking sparks) and the finale shell. Shape and motion live in `firework_spark.gdshader`: `start()` makes the shape from the particle index and RANDOM_SEED, `process()` applies gravity and drag, cools white to the colour to orange and sets the size. The draw shader turns each spark to the camera, stretches it along its own motion and keeps it at least 2.4 px; sparks are additive with HDR 12 to 16 so the camera bloom picks them up.
- **At each burst:** one `OmniLight3D` from a pool of four (colour of the set, energy 500+340xsize, range 620+200xsize, no shadow, fades over about 1.5 s), one flash billboard for 0.3 s, one smoke puff and the sound.
- **Global `g_fire`** (rgb = strongest burst colour, a = strength) is registered in `Fireworks.add_globals()`, called in `main.gd` next to `Grass.add_globals()` before any shader loads. The smoke draw shader and `water.gdshader` read it; the water gets a small glittery emission and the smoke is lit by the burst. Session A's sky shader does not read it (request below).
- **Smoke:** six pooled emitters, 42 puffs each, lifetime 24 s, drifting with the wind (global `g_wind` x1.7 high up and growing to 42 m). It is grey and lit by `g_fire`; the alpha is low so it reads as haze, not as a grey box.
- **Sound:** synthesised at start-up (`AudioStreamWAV`, 22050 Hz): launch whoosh, thump with a low rumble (a bigger one for the finale), crackle (150 tiny snaps) and a close-burst rumble. Two buses: `FireworksG` sends into the existing `Ground` bus (3D sources at the burst, heard by the pilot at home with distance attenuation and a lowpass) and `FireworksM` sends into `Onboard` (2D sources, gain from the drone distance, so the Listening position setting works). Every sound is scheduled after distance/343 s, so the light comes first. A burst nearer than 90 m to the drone adds a rumble on the microphone.
- **Shows:** `_build_shows()` makes three deterministic shows with a seeded RNG: 1) build up, 180 s, single gold shells, then mixed colours, then a dense last 20 s; 2) quiet night, 90 s, slow willow, ring and heart; 3) finale, 20 s barrage. A show is a list of events on a show clock that runs with the game and stops when the game is paused. The pause menu Weather tab has "Fireworks show": Off / Build up / Quiet night / Finale, and "Surprise shows": Off / On. A surprise show starts by itself at about 21:30 on every ninth day of the year (day of year mod 9 == 4), once per evening.
- **Night rule:** `request_show` will not start a show in daylight. If the sun is above -4 degrees it sets `hud.weather` to 21:30 on the same date (clock mode set, clock frozen), says "It is still light. The clock moves to half past nine." and starts the show as soon as it is dark.
- **Spark budget:** 6000 on desktop and 1500 on phones (`OS.has_feature("mobile")`; a laptop touch screen no longer counts). Two emitters per type at 300/300/340/220/220/240/420/440 sparks, 16 shell trails of 40 and 6 smoke puffs of 42 (about 5850 sparks at the peak).
- **Test hooks** (in a `shot=` run): `fireworks<1..3|build|quiet|finale>` starts a show even in daylight, `fwskip<seconds>` jumps the show clock and fires the bursts that would be in the air at that moment, `fwwall` puts one burst of every type in a row 190 m ahead, `fwburst<0..7>`, `fwsmoke`, `nosmoke`, `fwmenu` presses the real Weather tab and Finale buttons, `dof<n>` sets the depth of field. The run prints `fireworks: {...}` (running, show time, sun elevation, pending sounds) and the audio peak of each bus.

The RTH now turns on the velocity heading (the correct reference). The true root of the broken turn was a stall-induced phugoid: the bank reduced the vertical lift, the altitude hold over-pitched, the plane climbed to a stall (speed 3 m/s, alpha past 12 degrees), the lift dropped and it dove, repeating. **Fixed with a direct angle-of-attack limiter in the cruise hold**: when alpha passes 9 degrees it pitches the nose down hard, which breaks the stall-phugoid so the plane now completes a full 360-degree banked turn (radius about 50 m vs a predicted 63 m).

## How real phones handle this, and what the notification layer now copies (lane/notify, session B, 2026-10-23)
Robert asked to note the way real apps and phone operating systems deal with multiple messages and keep a notification centre. Here is the model, and what of it is implemented in `game/phone_notes.gd`.
- **Real Android behaviour being copied:**
  1. A notification is a record owned by the app, with a channel (importance), a timestamp and a conversation thread, not just a floating banner.
  2. Messages from the same conversation are grouped. A heads-up banner shows only the newest while the group is young; the group carries a summary ("DroneBudz · N notifications") and can be expanded to the individual notifications.
  3. The heads-up banner is temporary, but the notification itself is not: after the banner times out it stays in the notification shade until the player dismisses it or clears it. It is not marked read by the banner going away.
  4. The shade is the notification centre: the group summary, the expandable list of individual notifications, exact timestamps, per-notification dismissal, and a Clear all action. Dismissing one decrements the group count; clearing removes the group from the shade.
  5. While anything is unread, a small persistent app indicator (the status bar icon on a phone) stays on screen after the banner has gone, with the count, and tapping it opens the centre.
  6. New messages in the same thread update the existing group instead of stacking banners forever; the group count goes up and the newest preview changes.
- **Implemented here:** each message is stored with a sender, title, body, urgency, id and the in-game clock time. The heads-up card is Material and grouped ("· N notifications", latest message, large avatar, expand chevron only for groups). The notification centre opens by tapping the card or pressing N, shows the DroneBudz group with a count, collapses to a summary with the latest line and expands to every message with its clock time, a dismiss X per message, right-click dismissal, and Clear all. A small persistent DroneBudz status pill with the unread count appears at the top right once the heads-up has gone, and opens the centre. Dismissing the heads-up with a right click removes only the banner, the notification stays in the centre. New messages while the centre is open update the group live. Nothing reappears after Clear all.
- **Deliberately not copied (kept out because Robert does not want other phone furniture):** notification sounds and vibration (the drone audio is the event sound), do not disturb, per-app channel settings, notification actions (reply, mark read), snooze, lock screen, status bar clock/battery, home indicator, device frame, app drawer. The only phone surfaces on screen are the heads-up banner, the shade and the small status pill.
- **Evidence:** docs/realism/notify/00 the grouped heads-up banner, 01 a single notification, 02 the notification centre expanded with six notifications, 03 the centre collapsed to the group summary, 04 the persistent status pill after the banner has gone.
- **Questions that follow from this:**
  - N16. Should the status pill show the unread count, or just the app icon like a real status bar icon?
  - N17. Should the centre auto-expand the group when there are only one or two messages, and stay collapsed when there are many (current default is expanded)?
  - N18. A right click on the heads-up banner dismisses only the banner and keeps the notification; is a swipe style gesture worth adding on touch, or is the current tap and keys enough?
