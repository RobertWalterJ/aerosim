AeroSim Desktop Edition: Technical Specification v2

Supersedes the Gemini draft. Changes are driven by a critique of feasibility, physical correctness and what can be verified in a browser build.

2. Architecture

Three.js 0.170 with TypeScript and Vite on WebGL2. Layout: src/core, physics, world, rendering, audio, ui, game, util.

3. Physics

17-state quaternion rigid body, RK4. Four rotors with asymmetric motor lag and gyroscopic terms. LiPo battery with open-circuit voltage and internal-resistance sag. Eight penalty-spring contact points. Airframes: MINI (249 g, 3S, gimbal) and FREESTYLE (750 g, 4S, 25 degree fixed tilt). Headless tests cover static thrust, ground effect, translational lift, VRS, hover, braking, wind hold, acro rate and battery.

4. Environment

Victorian memorial park and valley: 4 km world, 2049 squared baked heightmap, land-cover and exact path distance textures, two terrain LODs plus an analytic backdrop ridge. Granite obelisks, headstones with engraved normal maps, lamps, benches, chapel with bell tower. Procedural spruce and broadleaf trees with hierarchical wind, rotor-downwash displacement and backlit translucency; GPU grass and leaf litter.

5. Lighting

Analytic sky, 4000 K sun, PMREM skylight, cascaded shadow maps, exponential height fog with forward scatter toward the sun.

6. Sensor pipeline (7 stages)

Metering and auto-exposure with 0.35 s lag; dual-filter bloom; rolling shutter (12 ms) and jello; Brown-Conrady distortion with chromatic aberration; sun streak, ghost orb, veiling glare, vignette and ISO noise; 10-stop S-curve; ISP sharpening halos; H.264-style macroblock artifacts driven by an RF link model.

7. Audio and ecology

Four motors with five harmonics each, vortex slap, wind buffet, slapback. Chickadee, robin, blue jay and nuthatch follow calm, alert and flush states. Perspectives: Ground Binaural, Onboard Mic, Cinematic Hybrid.

8. Controls and OSD

Keyboard, mouse and gamepad or USB-HID transmitter. OSD shows mode, GPS, RF, battery, ISO and shutter, altitude, speeds, wind, gimbal and a minimap.

9. Status and Phase 2

Status: complete pipeline builds and renders; first-light captures verified headlessly. Calibration of sky, foliage and exposure is ongoing. Phase 2: soft shadows (PCSS), octahedral impostors, fences and hedges, WebGPU port, optional Unreal Engine port for true photogrammetry-grade assets.
